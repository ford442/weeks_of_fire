Slow ambient lullaby with detuned soft-rock bones, 65 BPM, drifting 4/4 with no clear downbeat. Upright bass bowed and plucked softly, mixed low and slightly flat, as if walking underwater. Brushed kit at half-time, mostly cymbal texture, snare barely present. Vibraphone with tremolo, occasionally missing a note like a tired hand. A detuned upright piano playing a music-box figure that winds down across the track. One clean electric guitar with heavy spring reverb entering only in the last third, no solo, just held notes. Field-recording bed throughout: windshield wipers on an irregular cycle, dashboard hum, FM needle drift, faint AM static swelling between phrases. No chorus and no lift — the arrangement never resolves. Half-whispered female lead vocal, close and sleepy, mixed slightly under the instruments and partially buried in static; dual-track the title phrase so a second flatter "radio" voice arrives a beat late, as if answering from the dashboard. Bridge drops to static and a single streetlamp buzz for eight bars. Warm, dark, tape-saturated mix with almost no high end. Total runtime about 4:10, fading rather than ending. Use my Rubella voice for the driver and a flatter doubled take for the radio.

## Production notes

- **Emotional arc.** Fog → hypnotic drift → the bridge nearly becomes clarity and refuses it → the car keeps going. No resolution by design; the fade is the ending.
