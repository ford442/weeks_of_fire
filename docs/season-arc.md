# Season arc — Weeks on Fire (one-pager)

Tone map, not a plot dump. EyeWash Station airs all of it and never explains any of it.

| Ep | Register | What the episode *is* |
|----|----------|------------------------|
| **1** | Glamour fire | House of mirrors, burning town, corset and ping-pong. The Two are bored and want more fire. Lives get swapped; mascara must not smudge. |
| **2** | Liminal night | Dashboard glow, Radio Voice in the static, wildflowers, a factory skull, the caffeinated melon. Studio Huddle seeds the HOA weather system. |
| **3** | Suburban Halloween | Black cat, laser snakes, skeleton union break, Pizza Guy on the porch, Monster Mash on the lawn, spooky telephone poles. The mundane does not flinch. |
| **4** | Bureaucracy afterglow | The morning after the portal. The HOA reclassifies cosmic horror as seasonal décor. Brad's bin is still the wrong blue. The Two stand at the back wall in black lace, non-voting. |
| **5** | *Candidate* — honest height | **The Long Way Up** + **No Other Human Sounds**. Riley Smith. One wordy episode per season. Score withheld until the saddle. The EyeWash transmitter stays offscreen. |

**Logline for Ep4:** *The morning after the portal, the HOA reclassifies cosmic horror as seasonal décor.*

Brad's tag: *The bin is still the wrong blue — and the purple light has a parking permit.*

Karen vs. Marguerite Flood (two bureaucracies, one unscheduled signal) is an EyeWash crossover for later, not this episode's spine.

## EyeWash diegesis (locked)

EyeWash Station **slides**.

- Characters may have it on in the background (diegetic TV, corner CRT, clubhouse set).
- We may cut to station cards, idents, and master control that the characters do not see (framing device).
- **Nobody comments on the frame.** No one looks at camera and names the network.
- Continuity Voice is never shown. Continuity Voice is never confirmed to be the Episode 2 Radio Voice.
- The series does not air an episode of itself on the station until a finale.

The transmitter tower and the Spooky Telephone Poles material want to connect. That is a later shoot, not a season-one explanation. Strongly recommend the tower exists; do not show Riley walking up to it in Episode 4.

---

## What does not belong in Episode 4

**The Long Way Up** is the wordy episode. It only works because every other episode is nearly silent. It is **not** a neighbor of Item Three. Park it as the Episode 5 candidate with Riley, the ridge, and *The Honest Height*.

Gallery Fog / Caffeinated Melon stays Episode 2-adjacent. Skeleton Union Break stays Episode 3. Link them; do not dump them into the HOA runtime.
