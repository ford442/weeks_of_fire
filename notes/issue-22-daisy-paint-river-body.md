**Daisy Bell expansion + shared wildflower paint (2026-09-05)**

Table notes from the Daisy theme pass, now on `main`.

Spine: two women who already have each other borrow a living bicycle, refuse a carriage they cannot afford, and waltz through a century that cannot hold still — then the field takes them back. Joke is still the bike. Experiment is still the cut.

**Unreal insert is not dessert.** Leave the whipped-cream ocean to the other video. Daisy bikes off the edge road into a riverside building that has the river flowing *inside*: old single-pane glass, trees against the windows, trickle under the tires, light going milky through wavy panes. Tandem stays a tandem. Climax window C11–C13 (or the 3:43 silence bite if street flips keep the peak). Not the series building, not the laundry leak, not the lion ocean.

**Paint rivulets** are shared dirt grammar for Daisy's field bookends *and* the mountain-lion meadow. Wet enamel (ultramarine, sap green, cadmium, alizarin) running on the ground **between** the wildflowers, never climbing the petals. Gravity-true. At any tideline the paint thins into the water as tint — no slick sitting on top. Worlds still do not mix: no tandem in the multi-lion clearing, no leash on Fleet Street.

Generate a **ground-only plate** first. If the model paints the blooms instead of the dirt, the plate failed.
