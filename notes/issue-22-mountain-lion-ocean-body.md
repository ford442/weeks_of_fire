**Mountain Lion Wildflower Field — the drag now goes through an ocean**

The pull runs **flowers → ocean → flowers again → clearing**. Flowers are the corridor, the sea
is the doorway, and whatever the cat wanted to show us is on the other side of it. The cat knew
that the whole time.

The useful part: **the ocean is the crossfade.** `Come Further` runs out as the field thins into
water, the 8–12s fade happens over open sea, `Here They Are` starts as the blooms come back on the
far side. No third bed to generate — the water sits exactly where the two takes were already going
to overlap. Runtime goes to ~2:20.

```
00:00 – 00:12  | A – Insistence                 | Come Further
00:12 – 00:32  | B – Deeper pull                | Come Further
00:32 – 00:52  | C – Painted architecture       | Come Further
00:52 – 01:08  | D – The field becomes the sea  | Come Further → fade starts
01:08 – 01:30  | E – The crossing               | crossfade → Here They Are
01:30 – 01:48  | F – Far shore, flowers return  | Here They Are
01:48 – 02:06  | G – They're already here       | Here They Are
02:06 – 02:20  | H – Light and the field        | Here They Are
```

**How the water behaves** (locked, because this is where it can go wrong): no swimming, no
drowning, no peril · the flowers *become* the water instead of stopping at it — petals thin to
foam, the last lupines stand in surf, the tideline reads as one substance · we cross on something
that should not hold (a causeway of packed floating blooms) and it holds and nobody comments · the
lion is not impressed by the ocean, same walk, wet paws at most · the far shore is not the same
field, the flowers come back larger and more painted.

**Drag-corridor swap bank** — the structure is corridor → doorway → corridor → arrival, and the
corridor slot is reusable, so this cutaway can be re-cut later without re-boarding it: tall grass
at dusk · a parking structure the flowers are already winning · kelp forest at the surface line ·
a wheat field mid-wind · fog with shapes in it (once, never twice) · a dry riverbed of flat stones
· under a pier at low tide · snow with flowers coming up through it (save that one, it is a
different cutaway).

**Alternate destinations**, if the multi-lion clearing ever stops being the payoff — one per cut:
the other lions but they are *waiting on us* · one specific ordinary daisy, correctly sized ·
the physical seam where the photoreal field ends and the watercolor one begins · the ocean itself
was the destination and the far-shore flowers are a consolation · where the light comes from, and
it is just more field, brighter, with no explanation.

Quiet humor for the water: one shake-off at the worst moment for the camera · a blossom rides
alongside the whole crossing and beaches itself first · the leash floats and nobody adjusts.

**Shoot D and E first.** If the field and the water read as two pasted images instead of one
substance, that is the whole idea failing, and it should fail before anything else is generated.
