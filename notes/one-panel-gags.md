# One-Panel Gags — Newspaper-Comic Stills for Animation

Single-scene gag concepts in the spirit of a newspaper one-panel or *New Yorker* cartoon, pushed into the series' glamorous / bored / slightly dangerous / surreal register. Each is designed as **one striking still** that can later loop or move for 3–6 seconds in Grok Imagine.

**Tone lock:** dry, elegant, slightly menacing, absurd, or quietly apocalyptic. No broad slapstick. High-contrast cinematic light. Luxury materials against industrial or cosmic ones. Strong silhouettes. Negative space.

**Do not reuse:** the Ultra-Refreshed plant where the water is on fire; robot arms assembling something hidden in smoke and deep space; laser snakes; pizza-porch; HOA clipboard; hovering melon; technical-difficulties card.

**How to use:** generate the still from the Grok Imagine prompt; then animate with the suggested micro-motion only. Promote winners to `prompts/` as timed segments.

## 1. Closed for Cosmic Reasons

**Register:** grounded surreal with one impossible object.

**One-sentence visual:** Night hotel infinity pool; a laminated sign on a chrome stanchion reads CLOSED FOR COSMIC REASONS; the entire sheet of water hangs three feet above the empty basin, still rippling, still reflecting the city; underneath it a woman in a black maillot and heels sits dry on a deck chair, smoking, reading a magazine that should be soaked and isn't.

**The gag:** The pool is closed. She is using the shade.

**Suggested animation (3–6s):** Slow ripple travels across the hovering water; cigarette smoke rises and flattens against the underside like a ceiling; the ember pulses once; the magazine page does not turn.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
"Cinematic one-panel film still, night luxury hotel infinity pool, small laminated sign on a chrome stanchion reading CLOSED FOR COSMIC REASONS in polite institutional type, the entire rectangular sheet of dark water hovering three feet above the empty tiled basin still rippling and reflecting a distant city skyline, under the hovering water a bored glamorous woman in a black one-piece swimsuit and black heels sitting dry on a white deck chair smoking a thin cigarette, an open glossy magazine on her lap that is not wet, high-contrast moonlight and a single underwater pool light shining upward through the levitating water, strong silhouette, lots of negative night sky, photoreal, 35mm anamorphic, Grok Imagine style"

**Variations:** Swap the magazine for a room-service tray that is also dry. Or the sign is handwritten in lipstick.

## 2. The Moon Is Collect

**Register:** impossible.

**One-sentence visual:** Black-lacquer penthouse desk at night; a single red rotary phone ringing; its coiled cord runs out the open window and continues, taut and visible, all the way to a huge close moon; empty chair pulled back; lipstick-stained cigarettes in a crystal ashtray; a pair of black gloves left as if the owner just stepped away.

**The gag:** Someone is calling collect. The charges are astronomical. She stepped out.

**Suggested animation (3–6s):** The receiver trembles on each ring; the cord sways in high-altitude wind; the moon brightens a half-stop on the ring and dims between; one ash cylinder lengthens by a millimeter.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
"Cinematic one-panel film still, black lacquer penthouse interior at night, single red 1960s rotary telephone ringing on a desk, the coiled red cord running out an open floor-to-ceiling window and stretching taut across the sky all the way to a huge detailed full moon, empty leather chair pulled out, crystal ashtray of lipstick-stained cigarette ends, pair of black evening gloves abandoned on the blotter, city far below, high-contrast red plastic against black lacquer and silver moonlight, strong negative space, photoreal, 35mm, quiet menace, Grok Imagine style"

**Variations:** The moon has a tiny "NO CHARGES ACCEPTED" sticker. Or the gloves are still faintly steaming.

## 3. Third Shift Porcelain

**Register:** grounded surreal.

**One-sentence visual:** After-hours luxury department store; a line of couture mannequins at a factory time-clock; one has already punched out and stands in the loading-dock doorway smoking a cigarette made of chrome; banks of fluorescent tubes; a single red security-camera LED.

**The gag:** Beauty is hourly. Overtime is not discussed.

**Suggested animation (3–6s):** Time-clock thunks; a punch-card drops; chrome-cigarette smoke drifts; fluorescents flicker on one bank at a time; the camera LED blinks in 4/4.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
"Cinematic one-panel film still, after-hours luxury department store at night, row of pale fashion mannequins in black couture and pearls lined up at an industrial factory time-clock, one mannequin already clocked out standing in a loading-dock doorway smoking a perfectly chrome cigarette, punch-cards in a slot, marble floor, banks of cold fluorescent light, single red security-camera LED in the upper corner, high contrast, strong silhouettes, photoreal, 35mm, deadpan and slightly menacing, Grok Imagine style"

**Variations:** Their heels have time-clock stamps on the soles. Or one mannequin's smile is slightly more tired than the factory paint allows.

## 4. Cities in the Shell

**Register:** impossible.

**One-sentence visual:** Two-a.m. oyster bar, black marble, one Edison bulb; a woman in a sequined dress shucking; each opened shell holds a tiny, still-lit city; she squeezes lemon over one and the miniature streetlights flicker; she does not eat; she sets it aside like a rejected vintage.

**The gag:** Tonight's selection is a little metropolitan. She prefers something quieter.

**Suggested animation (3–6s):** A lemon bead falls in slow motion; one skyscraper window blinks out; tiny headlights crawl a grid; her red mouth almost smiles and doesn't.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
"Cinematic one-panel close-to-medium film still, 2am luxury oyster bar, black marble counter, single warm Edison bulb, glamorous bored woman with red lips and a dark sequined dress shucking oysters with a silver knife, each open shell contains a tiny glowing night city with lit windows and microscopic traffic, she is squeezing a lemon wedge over one city, a small pile of rejected city-oysters to her left, high contrast, luxury materials, photoreal macro detail in the shells, 35mm, quiet apocalypse, Grok Imagine style"

**Variations:** One oyster contains only a dark ocean. She keeps that one.

## 5. Complaint Window, Deep Field

**Register:** impossible.

**One-sentence visual:** A lone wood-and-brass customer-service window floating in deep space against a Hubble deep field; a woman in pearls slides a triplicate form through the slot; only the clerk's hands and a red-ink stamp are visible; the stamp reads DENIED; the ticket display says NOW SERVING: — and her number is 000000.

**The gag:** The universe has a wait time. It is not taking new tickets.

**Suggested animation (3–6s):** Stamp comes down; the form's edges lift as if in a draft that vacuum shouldn't have; stars drift; the window's fluorescent tube flickers; her earring rotates slowly in zero-g.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
"Cinematic one-panel film still, a single old-fashioned wood and brass customer-service window floating alone in deep space, Hubble deep-field galaxies behind it, a glamorous woman in a black dress and pearls in microgravity sliding a triplicate carbon form through the slot, only a clerk's pale hands and a red rubber stamp visible inside, the stamp just marking DENIED, a ticket display reading NOW SERVING: em dash, her paper ticket printed 000000, high contrast practical window light against cosmic black, strong silhouette, photoreal, 35mm anamorphic, dry bureaucratic dread, Grok Imagine style"

**Variations:** The clerk stamps PLEASE STAND BY. Or her form is a seating chart.

## 6. The Overnight Conservator

**Register:** grounded surreal.

**One-sentence visual:** Museum classical wing, 3 a.m.; a woman in a torn ballgown dusts a marble kouros; the statue holds her champagne flute; she holds its broken forearm like a clipboard and is initialing a condition report; hard museum spotlights; everything else velvet black.

**The gag:** She's restoring it. It's managing her evening.

**Suggested animation (3–6s):** Dust motes drift through the spotlight; one champagne bubble rises in the statue's glass; she turns a page; stone eyes catch the light as if they blinked — maybe they didn't.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
"Cinematic one-panel film still, classical sculpture gallery at 3am, a woman in a torn black ballgown and smeared red lipstick dusting a white marble kouros with a small conservator's brush, the statue calmly holding her champagne flute, she holding the statue's broken marble forearm like a clipboard and writing on a condition-report form, hard museum spotlight pools, deep velvet-black negative space, high contrast marble and silk, photoreal, 35mm, elegant and slightly dangerous, Grok Imagine style"

**Variations:** The condition report rates *her* for surface cracks. Or a second statue in the dark is holding her shoes.

## 7. Furnace Season

**Register:** grounded surreal.

**One-sentence visual:** A foundry converted for black-tie; a trough of molten iron pours in a slow orange ribbon behind a string quartet that will not look back; couture guests stand at a bored, safe distance; one woman in black lace warms her hands at the pour the way you warm them at a fireplace; sparks die on mink.

**The gag:** This is just how they do the weather now. Dress warm.

**Suggested animation (3–6s):** The pour never stops (perfect loop); sparks drift and extinguish; one violin bow draws slowly; heat shimmer; her fingers flex once toward the iron.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
"Cinematic one-panel film still, industrial iron foundry converted into a black-tie gala, a slow ribbon of molten iron pouring in the background, string quartet in evening wear playing without looking back, glamorous guests in couture standing at a bored distance, one woman in a torn black lace dress warming her hands at the pour as if it were a fireplace, orange molten light versus cold chrome and black silk, sparks dying on a mink stole, high contrast, strong silhouettes, photoreal, 35mm anamorphic, Grok Imagine style"

**Variations:** Place cards are fireproof. Or the quartet's rosin smoke matches the pour.

## 8. The Ice That Exhales

**Register:** grounded surreal with one material swap.

**One-sentence visual:** Banquet table, white linen, silver, one tall ice sculpture of a woman mid-curtsy; she is melting — but the melt is smoke, not water; the cloth beneath her is perfectly dry; a waiter in white gloves adjusts her a quarter-inch, as if this were still a centerpiece problem; lipstick on a napkin; empty chair.

**The gag:** She's been leaving the party all night. Nobody asked her to stay.

**Suggested animation (3–6s):** Smoke thickens from the sculpture's mouth and shoulders; the waiter's gloves make a micro-adjustment; candle flames lean away; one ice earring drops and becomes a puff.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
"Cinematic one-panel film still, formal banquet table with white linen and polished silver, a tall clear ice sculpture of an elegant woman mid-curtsy that is melting into dense cigarette-colored smoke instead of water, the tablecloth perfectly dry, a waiter in white gloves adjusting the sculpture a quarter inch, a lipstick-stained napkin, one empty chair, candlelight, high contrast, luxury and unease, photoreal, 35mm, Grok Imagine style"

**Variations:** The place card in front of the sculpture has her real name. Or the smoke spells nothing, which is worse.

## Production notes

- Generate **stills first**. Confirm the joke reads at thumbnail size (newspaper-comic test). If it needs a caption, the still isn't finished.
- Animation should never "sell" the joke harder. The motion is weather, machinery, or manners — not a punchline take.
- The Two can appear as silhouettes or red-lip close-ups (gags 1, 4, 7) but do not need to be named on screen.
- EyeWash Station can live in-frame as a tiny lobby TV (gags 1, 3, 6) without comment.
- Aspect: 4:5 or 3:2 for stills; 16:9 if the later move is a slow push-in.
