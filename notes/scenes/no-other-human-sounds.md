**Slugline:** EXT. FOREST ROAD 4N22 — UNMAINTAINED CUT-THROUGH — 5:40 PM THROUGH 6:25 PM
**Runtime target:** 6–8 min (≈7 script pages)
**Music cue:** **None** until the sedan is audible again at the fork. Needles underfoot. Cloth. One breath that is slightly too loud. If a later draft scores the woods, the later draft is wrong.
**Status:** draft — Episode 5 candidate insert, after Long Way Up Beat E (boots / hose clamp), before they drive to Pulpit Overlook
**Dialog versions (alts):** [`versions/no-other-human-sounds-versions.md`](versions/no-other-human-sounds-versions.md)

**Cast:** RUBELLA VALE (walks) · RILEY SMITH (walks) · LILLITH VALE (threshold + reunion; silent for the woods)

**Spine:** The sedan will get down, not up. Lillith takes it the graded fork. Rubella and Riley walk the unmaintained cut-through. The forest is so still that talking costs something. They talk anyway — a steady rally, short lines, no engine to hide in. They come out. They get in. They drive to the cliff.

## The Joke (read this before writing a single line)

*The Long Way Up* is the wordy episode: eleven minutes of cable-procedural dialog on a gold hillside. This scene is what happens when that episode remembers it is a silent show, and then two people who do not play the same game accidentally start a rally anyway.

They step off the graded fire road into a closed-canopy stretch the county stopped maintaining in 2011. No wind. Afternoon birds already done. The sedan gone the long way. For the first time all day there is no engine to hide a sentence in.

The comedy is **the cost of a line**, then **the rally**:

- Every speech has to earn its noise against a wood that will not help.
- Riley answers the question asked, then the number. Biography leaks only through the adjacent question.
- Rubella, freshly corrected in the car and unmarked about it, asks better questions and does not mention the lecture.
- Once the last name is Smith, they settle into a back-and-forth that sounds like The Two except one of them keeps being correct.
- The last name is Smith. Rubella finds this aggressively adequate.

**What this scene is not:** a confession walk. Nobody says "I've never told anyone this." Nobody cries. The pendant is fourteen dollars. Thanksgiving happened. If a line could be delivered by two people on a soap-opera trail holding mugs, cut it. Every sincere line must be load-bearing on a distance, a mineral, a filing system, a boot, or a cliff.

**Emotional notes:** Warmth arrives through logistics, same rule as the parent scene. The rally is how they become slightly more themselves — not disclosure, just rhythm. The eight-second stop is written, timed, and non-negotiable. The engine returning is the first other human sound and it is a relief neither of them names.

**Hard rules:**
- **No other human sounds** between Lillith's threshold line and the sedan at the fork. No Continuity Voice. No radio. No Lillith calling into the woods.
- **Do not revisit the pseudoscience correction.**
- **Do not explain "Around."** Do not have Rubella ask.
- **Do not show the tower.** Pulpit Overlook is a cliff and a valley. The antenna is still around a bend.
- **No score in the woods.** Needles, cloth, breath. The idle at the fork is the first machine.
- **Riley is never wrong about a fact and never smug about it.**
- **The car goes to the cliff. It does not go to the tower.** Down, not up. That was the finding under the hood.

### B — 5:48 PM — The Room with the Door Shut

```
EXT. FOREST ROAD 4N22-C — UNMAINTAINED CUT-THROUGH — LATE AFTERNOON

The canopy closes. The gold world ends like a door.

An old dirt track, one car wide, unmaintained, the crown so
soft with needles that their feet make a sound like paper,
not gravel. Black oak. Ponderosa. A downed limb the road went
around instead of through. No wind. No birds. No cicadas —
those were a south-face animal and this is not the south face.

RUBELLA walks as if the woods asked her to keep it down.
RILEY walks the way she walked the center line: like the
mileage is a problem she is solving.

They go twenty seconds with no dialog. Footsteps. Cloth.
Rubella's stiletto finding dirt under the needles and
disliking it without comment.

```
RUBELLA

RILEY
The woods.

RUBELLA

There is always a paren b.

## Production Notes

**Runtime target:** 6–8 min. This is not a second wordy episode. The new pages are a rally of short lines, not another exposition act. If Beat E (the rally) starts sounding like the car, cut it back to turns.

**Placement:** After *The Long Way Up* Beat E (hood up / the boots / it will get down, not up) and before Beat F (switchbacks). Sequence: fill the radiator → fork → Lillith takes the graded road down and west → Rubella and Riley walk 4N22-C → rally → rejoin → drive four-tenths to **Pulpit Overlook** → walk the rest from the cliff side. The sedan stays at the overlook. It does not attempt the tower.
