*Companion to [`../the-long-way-up.md`](../the-long-way-up.md). Baseline stays

or differently wrong-genre pages.*

**Parent scene:** The Long Way Up (Ep4 dialog anchor)  
**Register target:** Cable procedural → chatty sci-fi (wordy episode)  
**Character inventory:** [`../../../characters/riley-smith.md`](../../../characters/riley-smith.md)

---

## Juxtaposition (why the wordiness exists)

Same family as:

- **Monster Mash (1958)** blasted on a lawn still littered with laser snakes —
  full commitment to a party single that does not belong in the frame.
- **Rubella's monkey science** — the ground-current / rats lecture delivered
  like cable documentary fact, until someone (here, Riley) is finally correct
  at it.
- **This scene:** somebody sat on the remote. The silent silly show becomes a
  **mid-season procedural with act breaks** about three women and a hill. The
  cast does not notice. They just keep talking.

**The wordiness must stand out.** If you can cut half the lines and lose only
plot, you cut the wrong half. Cut sentiment; keep volume, structure, and
over-specification. Interpersonal pages (V2–V3) still earn their noise — no
patio-mug confessions that could float free of a mile marker, a padlock, a
car in Tulare, or a sister who stood up first at lunch.

Station card stays non-negotiable in every version that opens the episode:

```
        THE FOLLOWING PROGRAM CONTAINS DIALOGUE.
```

## Keeper lines (every full version preserves or consciously breaks)

Preserve unless the version header says **breaks keepers**:

1. `You didn't stop me. I'm walking. You decelerated.` (or a clear upgrade that still splits two legal events)
2. Lions **six-forty**, seasonal; truck math → **three** vehicles a day
3. Tower year: **ninety-eight** / funding vote **ninety-six**
4. Car is **in Tulare** (running status refused or deferred)
5. Later in parent: hose clamp not head gasket; one boot / one stiletto; fourteen-second saddle silence
6. Riley never smug; never wrong about a fact

**Allowed to die** (parent teaser lines the room is tired of):

- `Do you know why we stopped you?` as the real open (may survive only as Lillith irony)
- Bare `Get in the car` / ride-offer grammar without a contract or a read

## V1 — Cold open (no traffic-stop cliché)

**Status:** alt  
**Beats covered:** A  
**Register:** Deadpan / observational  
**What it changes:** Removes "do you know why we stopped you" and ride-offer
grammar. Keeps decelerated + vehicle math + tower years.  
**Risks:** Slightly less "wrong genre" unless the station card and crawl pace
still sell procedural.

### A — 2:10 PM — We Don't Usually Slow Down

```
EXT. RIDGE ROAD — DAY

(Station card. Sedan matches walking pace. Same picture as parent.)

LILLITH
We've been behind you for a mile. You haven't looked back once.
That's either confidence or a woman who already knows what's back there.

RILEY
If it mattered, it'd pass me.

LILLITH
We don't usually slow down for anyone. You made us curious.
That's a problem. Curiosity is how afternoons get expensive.

RILEY
You didn't stop me. I'm walking. You decelerated. Those are two
different events with two different paperwork trails.

LILLITH
(quiet, to Rubella)
She's good.

RUBELLA
Ma'am — four vehicles a day on this road, two of them the same truck,
lions at six-fifteen.

RILEY
Six-forty. Seasonal. Water hauler for the pistachio operation, same
truck twice, so three vehicles a day, safest road in the county on a
per-capita basis.

LILLITH
Rubella.

RUBELLA
I heard it.

LILLITH
Where are you headed.

RILEY
Kestrel Ridge repeater. Weather antenna. Decommissioned ninety-eight.

RUBELLA
Ninety-six.

RILEY
Ninety-eight. Ninety-six was the funding vote. They ran two more
seasons on money that was technically already spent. Most American
fact about that tower.

RUBELLA
We're going that way. You're going to argue with the empty seat
or you're going to use it.

RILEY
That's still not how you offer somebody a ride.

LILLITH
We aren't offering a ride. We're admitting we can't leave you alone.
Rarer. Don't make it sentimental. Get in.
```

### Notes

- Door open = end of teaser, same as parent.
- "Afternoons get expensive" plants Vale boredom without a monologue.

## V2 — Mulholland Falls crawl (interpersonal / power)

**Status:** active  
**Beats covered:** A–B  
**Register:** Neo-noir competence theater; short clean cruelty  
**What it adds:** "We" as a corporation; arrangement/contract; lunch that ended
early; Lillith as detective who charges for implications.  
**Risks:** Can go hard-boiled past comedy — keep Riley flat and correct.

### A — 2:10 PM — The Arrangement

```
EXT. RIDGE ROAD — DAY

(Station card. Crawl.)

LILLITH
Ma'am.

(Riley keeps walking.)

LILLITH (CONT'D)
We saw you from the bend. We don't usually see anyone from the bend.

RILEY
That's a lot of we for two people.

RUBELLA
It's efficient.

RILEY
It's a marriage without the paperwork.

(Beat. Rubella almost smiles. Lillith does not.)

LILLITH
Sisters. You implied. Implication is a kind of asking. I charge for both.

RILEY
I didn't ask.

LILLITH
You diagnosed. Same invoice.

(Riley still has not looked at them for more than a glance.)

LILLITH (CONT'D)
She drives. I decide. That's the arrangement. If you get in, you're
in our arrangement for the duration of the hill.

RILEY
And if I don't like the arrangement.

RUBELLA
Then you walk. You've already proven you can. We're offering a more
interesting mistake.

LILLITH
Don't romanticize her. She's offering air conditioning and an
interrogation. Those are not the same gift.

RILEY
You didn't stop me. I'm walking. You decelerated —

LILLITH
— two events, two paperwork trails. We heard. You're good. Stay good
and answer the next one: where are you headed.

RILEY
Kestrel Ridge repeater. Decommissioned ninety-eight.

RUBELLA
Ninety-six.

RILEY
Funding vote ninety-six. Tower ninety-eight. Spent money walking
around for two years pretending it was still a budget. American.

RUBELLA
Lions clock in at six-fifteen. Four vehicles a day.

RILEY
Six-forty. Three vehicles. Math is not a vibe.

LILLITH
Get in the car.

RILEY
That's not how you —

LILLITH
It's how a contract starts. Get in.
```

### B — 2:40 PM — The Interview (Falls cut)

```
INT. SEDAN — MOVING — DAY

Riley in back, pack belted like a passenger. Lillith fully turned around.
Detective who has decided to be casual about it.

LILLITH
Full name.

RILEY
Riley.

LILLITH
Half a name. Famous or avoiding process.

RILEY
Not famous.

LILLITH
So process. What did you leave in Tulare besides a car that may or
may not run.

RILEY
A brother who texts about parts. A mother who texts about weather.
A garage apartment that does not text.

LILLITH
Boyfriend.

RILEY
No.

LILLITH
Girlfriend.

RILEY
No.

LILLITH
Then what are you doing on a three-car road at the worst hour of the
worst month looking like a woman with somewhere to be and nowhere
to be at the same time.

RILEY
I'm doing a site. That's a place. Places are not people. You're
confusing categories.

RUBELLA
She's always confusing categories. It's how she flirts.

LILLITH
Weather antenna. Hobby, job, or personality disorder.

RILEY
Hobby that used to be a job. Site surveys. Nine years. You drive to
hardware nobody has thought about since Clinton and write down whether
it's still there. It is almost always still there. That's the finding.
Nobody takes anything down. Taking it down costs money. Leaving it up
costs nothing. The country accumulates.

RUBELLA
Most depressing sentence anyone has said in this car.

LILLITH
It's two-forty in the afternoon.

RUBELLA
I'm aware of the time, Lillith.

LILLITH
Follow-up. Eleven point something miles, pack, August, two PM —
car behind you in a condition you won't describe, or no car.

RILEY
Car's in Tulare.

LILLITH
Is it running.

RILEY
It's in Tulare.

LILLITH
(to the windshield)
She's got a whole thing.

RUBELLA
Everyone's got a whole thing.

LILLITH
We don't.

RUBELLA
We have the biggest thing anyone in this car has ever had.

LILLITH
Funeral or wedding — the lace. She's going to ask eventually.
I'll save her the breath. Neither. We dress for the day we meant
to have, not the day we got.

RILEY
You had a lunch.

RUBELLA
It ended.

RILEY
Badly?

LILLITH
Early. Bad has a story. Early means somebody stood up first.

RILEY
Who stood up first.

RUBELLA
(eyes on the road)
She always thinks it was me.
```

### Notes

- Promote A+B together if Falls wins; don't mix V2 open with V0 bare "get in."
- "Categories" callback pays off when Riley corrects the science lecture later.

## V3 — Daytime triangulation (soap heat, load-bearing)

**Status:** active  
**Beats covered:** A–B  
**Register:** Daytime cross-exam + sister poison; still anti-mug  
**What it adds:** Who Riley answers first; pendant/lace swap; Thanksgiving
contrast; Lillith policing Rubella's bonding.  
**Risks:** Softness — every sincere beat must still hitch to a fact.

### A — 2:10 PM — Pulses Get Interviews

```
EXT. RIDGE ROAD — DAY

RUBELLA
You're in the middle.

RILEY
So is the paint. The paint's legal.

LILLITH
Paint doesn't have a pulse. You do. Pulses get interviews.

RILEY
You didn't stop me. I'm walking. You decelerated. Two events.
Two trails. I'm not on either of yours yet.

LILLITH
Yet is a flirt in a procedural, darling. Don't.

(Quiet beat. Heat shimmer.)

RUBELLA
Four vehicles. Two the same truck. Lions at six-fifteen.

RILEY
Three vehicles. Six-forty. Seasonal. Per-capita, this is the safest
road in the county. You're the anomaly.

LILLITH
(to Rubella, pleased against her will)
She's good.

RUBELLA
I heard it.

LILLITH
Where.

RILEY
Kestrel Ridge. Repeater. Ninety-eight.

RUBELLA
Ninety-six —

RILEY
— was the vote. Don't make me do the American budget joke. I'll do
it if you force me.

RUBELLA
Do it in the car. The empty seat is losing an argument with the heat.
```

### B — 2:40 PM — Who She Answers

```
INT. SEDAN — MOVING — DAY

LILLITH
I'm speaking to you.

RILEY
You're speaking at the car. She asked a question with a number in it.

RUBELLA
(quiet, pleased)
I did.

LILLITH
I ask better questions.

RILEY
You ask more questions. Different metric.

LILLITH
Okay. Metric. Job.

RILEY
Was. Site surveys. Nine years of findings. Finding is: the country
accumulates dead hardware because removal has a line item and neglect
doesn't.

LILLITH
Family.

RILEY
Alive. Local. Fine. Thanksgiving happened.

LILLITH
Nobody says Thanksgiving happened unless they're making a point.

RILEY
I'm making a contrast. You two talk like a closed loop. Closed loops
assume everyone else is broken open. Some people have a table and a
pie and a brother who takes the leftover foil. Car's in his driveway.
Tulare. Timing chain. Part in Fresno until Wednesday. I walked because
Wednesday is after the ducting window and a rental is a desk with wheels.

RUBELLA
We don't do desks.

RILEY
I noticed. Desks would smudge.

LILLITH
The purple. Meaningful or decorative.

RILEY
Amethyst. Fourteen dollars. Bishop. It hangs. Gravity's the whole
biography. What does the lace do.

(Beat. First question that isn't a correction.)

LILLITH
It tells a room we already know how the room ends.

RILEY
And if the room doesn't end.

RUBELLA
Then we look expensive while we wait.

LILLITH
You're bonding. I hate when you bond without filing a report.

RUBELLA
Report: she answers numbers first, names second, and us not at all
until we earn it.

RILEY
You haven't earned it. You've matched pace. That's hospitality or
a stakeout. I'll know which by the gate.
```

### Notes

- "I'll know which by the gate" hands off cleanly to Beat C.
- Pendant at fourteen dollars must stay consistent with *No Other Human Sounds*.

## V4 — Ultra-wordy cable (maximum juxtaposition)

**Status:** active  
**Beats covered:** A–B; optional C patch  
**Register:** Mid-season network drama that will not shut up  
**What it adds:** Volume as the gag — overlapping concerns, recap grammar,
people narrating their own procedures.  
**Risks:** Fatigue. If using V4, protect later silences harder; do not also
densify the woods insert.

### A — 2:10 PM — Cold Open Recap Energy

```
EXT. RIDGE ROAD — DAY

(Station card holds an extra half-second. Ugly.)

LILLITH
For the record — and I am making a record, even if the only stylus
is the heat — we are not lost, we are not hiking, and we are not
in the habit of decelerating for strangers on a county line with
no shoulder.

RUBELLA
She means hello.

LILLITH
I mean inventory. Name.

RILEY
Riley.

LILLITH
Direction of travel.

RILEY
Ridge.

LILLITH
Purpose.

RILEY
Site.

LILLITH
Affiliation.

RILEY
None that still cuts a check.

RUBELLA
Lions six-fifteen, four vehicles —

RILEY
Six-forty, three vehicles, water hauler counted twice is still one
truck, pistachio north side, seasonal cats, per-capita safest,
you decelerated, I did not stop, paperwork would hate this sentence
as much as I do.

LILLITH
She's good.

RUBELLA
She's exhausting. I respect it.

LILLITH
Tower.

RILEY
Kestrel Ridge weather repeater, one-ninety of lattice on five-two
hundred of ridge, decommissioned ninety-eight, funding theater
ninety-six, dead power, dead feed, geometry still employed. If you
want the lecture on ducting, you'll get it when the road gets dirt
and somebody turns into a whiteboard without a whiteboard.

RUBELLA
That somebody is going to be her.

LILLITH
Get in. Not a favor. A jurisdiction. For the next vertical mile
you are in our show, and our show just became the kind with acts.
```

### B — 2:40 PM — Act One Interview (network cut)

```
INT. SEDAN — MOVING — DAY

LILLITH
Act One questions are biographical. Don't look for the commercial;
there isn't one until the gate.

RILEY
You narrate your own structure.

RUBELLA
We have to. Nobody else will.

LILLITH
Hobby that used to be a job — expand. Use full sentences. This
episode paid for full sentences.

RILEY
I drove to hardware left over from administrations people romanticize
and I wrote down whether the hardware still existed. It did. It does.
Nine years. The finding is neglect scales. Removal is a budget.
Existence is free. Free things bury a landscape.

RUBELLA
Write that on a mug and I will leave the marriage.

LILLITH
We are not a marriage.

RILEY
You keep saying we like a merger filing.

LILLITH
Car.

RILEY
Tulare. Brother's driveway. Timing chain. Not the head gasket —
people love a head gasket; it's dramatic. Chains are just true.

LILLITH
Money.

RILEY
Month-to-month garage in Three Rivers. Contract rebid. Desk in
Bakersfield offered, declined. A desk is the opposite of the job.

RUBELLA
We declined worse things for worse reasons.

LILLITH
Lunch.

RUBELLA
It ended early.

LILLITH
She stood up first.

RUBELLA
She always thinks —

LILLITH
I keep score. Scoring is love with a clipboard.

RILEY
Thanksgiving happened. Alive, local, fine. I'm not a wound you can
interview open. If you need a wound, invent one after the act break.
I have a padlock and a grade and a hose clamp in my future and none
of them care who left lunch.
```

### C — optional patch — Jury reads the whole sign

If V4 is in, let the ordinance be read **aloud in full** (parent already has
the text on the sign). Extra wordiness = reading paren a/b/c until Rubella
opens the gate out of spite.

```
LILLITH
(reading, committed, ugly)
"Seasonal closure — Forest Road 4N22 — vehicle access prohibited
May 15 through November 1 per ordinance twelve dash one fourteen
paren b — violators subject to citation — foot and equestrian
traffic permitted."

RUBELLA
Paren a.

RILEY
Definitions.

LILLITH
Paren c.

RILEY
Enforcement discretion. There is always enforcement discretion.
It's how cut locks get re-hung so a moving vehicle feels legal.
```

### Notes

- V4 is the purest **Monster Mash move**: wrong show, full volume.
- Pair with parent Beats E–H unchanged so silence at the saddle still detonates.
