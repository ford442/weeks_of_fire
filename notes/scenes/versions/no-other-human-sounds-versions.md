## V1 — Rally-first (trim biography)

**Status:** alt  
**Beats:** Collapse C–D; land on E sooner  
**What it changes:** Less interview residue from the car; more pure rhythm.

```
RUBELLA
Your turn.

RILEY
Why did you get out of the car.

RUBELLA
It had expressed itself.

RILEY
That's a mechanical opinion, not a reason.

RUBELLA
I don't like being the one who waits.

RILEY
You waited in lace on a grade. Waiting's a skill.

RUBELLA
I don't collect skills. I collect exits.

RILEY
Then this is a poor trail for you. One way out and it's up.
```

## V2 — Interpersonal bleed (short turns only)

**Status:** fragments  
**Rule:** If a line needs a paragraph, it belongs on the ridge, not here.

```

RILEY

I'm practicing.

RILEY

line. It's not mine.
```

```
RUBELLA
Apparently.
```
