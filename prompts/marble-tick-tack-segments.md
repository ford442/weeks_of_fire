# Marble Tick Tack — Segment Prompts

Copy-ready prompts for **Grok Imagine** (start stills + image-to-video) and **Gemini Omni** (short video).
Song: `songs/Marble_Tick_Tack.md` (~2:03 instrumental). Visible on the site **Suggestions** view.

**Prompt policy:** written *long on purpose* — near the practical Grok Imagine
prompt ceiling (~1,000–1,300 characters). One continuous run: subject → magnet
bodies → blocking / job → dancer bank (three or four, never all) → room layers →
practical light → lens / grade → style tag. Do not trim them to “clean them up.”
If a generation comes back mushy, cut **background clutter** first, never the
job (orbit, jump, gears, tumble, freeze) or the dancer that proves the room.

**Imagine rules:** one job per clip. Continuous single take, no cut, no mid-clip scene swap. Station objects hop in place. Magnets may travel. No faces, no lips, no creatures, no building-cast. Camera follows one snap. “Camera not moving” only when the shot is a hold. Replace native audio with `marble_tick_tack.mp3`.

## Visual arc

Cabinet marble chorus → cube slide to glasses → doorway onto dirt alley → copper Rodin torus → spheres in the ruts → fence gap onto unreal emerald lawn → electromagnet tower rise/slump → frozen ferrofluid rain at the dirt/grass seam → thaw and smash → slick spheres home past the dry Rodin → cabinet chain lock → 3s decay.

## Magnetics pass (2026-09-05)

Board: [`ideas/marble-tick-tack-magnetics.md`](../ideas/marble-tick-tack-magnetics.md). Prior pass:
[`ideas/marble-tick-tack-orbit.md`](../ideas/marble-tick-tack-orbit.md).

Three additions, one rule.

- **Cylinders are now a body.** Discs stand on their flat ends and spin like tops; rods roll on
  their sides and pair end-to-end into tumbling dumbbells. Discs and rods do things spheres cannot.
- **The jump gap.** A magnet holds still across a visible gap, then crosses it in one frame and
  slams into its partner. Lands on the **68 BPM kick**, never on an eighth. One per clip.
- **The dancer bank.** Many more objects vibrating on the eighths so the *rooms* move, not just
  the props.

**Rule: dancers hop, magnets orbit.** Furniture never travels. If a fence slat starts orbiting
something, kill the take.

### Jobs vocabulary

| Job | Reads as | Fails as |
|-----|----------|----------|
| Cyclotron orbit | Spheres lap the Rodin or a coil at 136 RPM | A marble bowl |
| Magnetic gears | Two bodies counter-rotate, never touching | Contact; grinding |
| **Mutual orbit** | Two equal bodies rotate around the **empty point between them** | One big thing with a moon |
| **End-over-end tumble** | A rod pair rotates around its shared middle while travelling | A stick being carried |
| **Top spin + precession** | A disc on its flat end spins at 136 RPM, axis wobbling slowly | A coin settling |
| **Jump gap** | Still, then one-frame cross, then slam | A roll; a cut; a throw |
| **Rebound ring** | Pair buzzes together and settles over four eighths | A bounce; a dead stop |
| **Counter-rotating stack** | Alternating discs in a column spin opposite ways | The whole column spinning |
| **Standing vibration** | Bars buzz in place hard enough to blur, going nowhere | Sliding; falling |
| Almost | Millimeter gap, freeze | Any orbit, any jump |

### How to make a jump read

Show the gap for a full beat first · both bodies dead still before it · cross on one frame with a
streak, nothing in between · land the impact on the kick · pay it with a grit puff and a rebound
ring · never cut inside the jump · one per clip.

### Dancer bank

Pick three or four per clip, never all of them. Indoors they are glass and metal, in the alley
dry and gritty, on the lawn wet — that is how the rooms stay apart when the magnets are doing the
same job in all three.

**Cabinet** — marbles hopping in the grooves · wine-glass rims ringing without moving · speaker
cones pumping · a hanging pull-chain ticking on the cabinet side · washers loose on a nail · a
saucer of water with crossing rings · ball bearings in a tin lid · a key ring on a hook · lamp
filament flickering a hair off the grid · shelf dust jumping in the lamp beam

**Alley** — fence slats shivering · frost ticking on wire · grit and gravel hopping in the ruts ·
chain-link buzzing like a screen · a loose corrugated sheet oil-canning on the kick · a bare bulb
swinging a few degrees · nails standing in a board · a hubcap ringing against the fence · dust
motes pulsing in the tower light

**Lawn** — dew beads jumping off the blades · flower heads nodding on the kick · grass tips
shivering on the eighths · coil housings humming visibly · standing water in a footprint with
concentric rings · a sprinkler head ticking · saturated petals shedding one at a time on the downbeat

## Geography

Three rooms on one night lot. Doorway of the cabinet room opens onto the alley. Alley opens onto the lawn. Lawn returns along the fence line to the same doorway. Red tower light stays in the far dark so the lot is one place.
