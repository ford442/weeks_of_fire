**Imagine rules:** one job per clip. Continuous single take, no cut, no mid-clip scene swap except the 6s regenerates (B, D) whose job *is* the inpaint. Station objects hop in place. Magnets may travel. Lion, aliens, London extras, and the flower bicycle hop only — no lips, no acting, no hunt, no building-cast. Camera follows one snap. “Camera not moving” only when the shot is a hold. 10s clips hold one world. Replace native audio with `marble_tick_tack.mp3`.

Cabinet in A and N/O must be the same warm-lamp wooden display cabinet as night-lot Marble Tick Tack. A lost cabinet means the souvenirs cannot come home.
