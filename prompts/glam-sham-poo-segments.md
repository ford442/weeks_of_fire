## Visual arc

Kenji hero pitch in neon Glamora → laundry-room dual frontwomen → fluorescent office salaryman panic → product bottle hold → club glam → logo / vending-machine end card.

The **original 5-still commercial is the spine**. The fluorescent office is an intercut that only exists on the shouting samples — do not let it take over the package.

## Character continuity

- **Kenji "Shred" Sato** — enormous teased hair, leather fringe, always mid-pitch, noise meter already in the red
- **Rubella** (right) — dry, exhausted cabaret, deadpan; metal hair she did not ask for
- **Lillith** (left) — brighter, chaotic, fully committed to the bit
- **Palette** — Glamora magenta and cyan neon outdoors; institutional fluorescent green-white indoors
- **Register** — late-80s glam metal treated as a real cosmetic ad; the ad never winks

## Lipsync rule

Stills pipeline, so **beat-sync over mouth shapes**. Keep singers off-camera or non-mouthing for verses — performance and attitude only. On-camera mouths are allowed **only** on the gang chant "GLAM-SHAM-POO!" Cuts land on chorus hits, not syllables.
