## Audio lock (2026-09-05)

Bed is **Ultra Screech 2** with the opening scream from the first take spliced on:
`Ultra_Screech_2_with_scream.mp3` — **~2:51**, 128 BPM.

## Visual lock

The **original 5-still commercial** (`glam_sham_poo_commercial_clip.mp4`) is the spine and
the preferred look. Glam volume stays up — big teased hair, not cartoon beehives. The
hair-dialed-back redo is **not** primary; the v2 office sequence is an alternate that only
gets picture on the shouting samples.

Kenji hero pitch → laundry-room dual frontwomen → office panic intercut → product bottle →
club glam → logo / vending-machine end card. Everything new below hangs off that spine.

## Character continuity

- **Kenji "Shred" Sato** — enormous teased hair, leather fringe, always mid-pitch, noise meter already in the red
- **Rubella** (right) — dry, exhausted cabaret, deadpan; metal hair she did not ask for
- **Lillith** (left) — brighter, chaotic, fully committed to the bit
- **Salarymen** — optional, fluorescent, angry, never named
- **Palette** — Glamora magenta and cyan neon outdoors; institutional fluorescent green-white indoors
- **Register** — late-80s glam metal treated as a real cosmetic ad; the ad never winks

No real-actor names in any prompt. This is a recurring commercial interrupt, not an HOA
screenplay — it never grows a plot.

## Lipsync rule

Stills pipeline, so **beat-sync over mouth shapes**. Keep singers off-camera or
non-mouthing for verses — performance and attitude only. On-camera mouths are allowed
**only** on the gang chant "GLAM-SHAM-POO!" (segments E, H, J) and on Kenji's two short
spoken lines (A, J). Everything else cuts on chorus hits, not syllables.

## Tempo math

128 BPM → beat 0.469s, bar 1.875s, 4 bars 7.5s. Every mark below is bar-aligned from the
first downbeat after the scream. Seconds are nominal — conform to the waveform, keep the
bar counts.

## Joke-network on-screen gags

Graphics layer, added in the edit. Every one of these is a still-safe overlay — none of
them need a new generation, and none of them need the ad to admit it is a joke.

1. **Corner bug** — a small fluorescent **EyeWash Station** bug rides the whole spot, wrong
   aspect ratio, slightly too bright. It is the only thing on screen that never gets loud.
2. **Price flash on the bottle hold (E)** — `¥???  — ASK YOUR RA` slams on in commercial
   yellow, holds four bars, never resolves to a number.
3. **Legal crawl on the end card (J)** — *Results may attract laser snakes. Not responsible
   for citations issued under HOA Article 9 or campus noise curfew. Quiet Formula
   discontinued.* Scrolls too fast to finish reading, which is the joke.
4. **Live noise meter** — a broadcast-style dB meter pinned in the red in the lower right,
   present from B onward. On the outro it finally drops to zero, one beat after the music
   stops.
5. **Salaryman subtitle burn-in (D, I)** — the shouts get hard subtitles: "THIS IS NOT IN
   THE BUDGET" / "SALES TARGET NOT MET" / "WHO APPROVED THIS FORMULA?!" — each in a
   slightly different font, as if three people made this ad.
6. **Alumni disclaimer (G)** — one line under the handbook macro: *Glam-Sham-Poo College
   alumni claim immunity from HOA citations. The HOA has not agreed to this.*
7. **Sold-out sticker (J)** — a **FRESHMAN ORIENTATION SIZE — AVAILABLE AT MOVE-IN** starburst
   over the vending machine, with a smaller, sadder **QUIET FORMULA** sticker under it that
   just says *discontinued*.
8. **Station return card** — the last frame cuts to a plain fluorescent slate: **WE ALWAYS
   RETURN**, silent, holding one second too long, which hands the interrupt back to whatever
   it interrupted.

## Optional sting (3–6s)

**"GLAM-SHAM-POO!" gang-chant sting** — a reusable commercial interrupt for anywhere in the
series. Black, one gang chant, one logo flash, back to program. Cut it from the final
chorus of the 2:51 bed so it always matches the full spot.

```
00.0 – 00.4  | black, fluorescent hum bleeding in from whatever it interrupted
00.4 – 02.4  | one gang chant "GLAM-SHAM-POO!" over the logo card, magenta flash on the beat
02.4 – 03.6  | snap cut to the bottle, noise meter spikes into the red
03.6 – 04.4  | hard cut to black, the meter drops to zero one beat late
```

**Gemini Omni**
```
4-second sting: black, a magenta logo flash on a gang chant, a snap cut to a chrome heavy-metal shampoo bottle, hard cut to black. Broadcast noise meter in the corner spikes then drops. No mouths, no lipsync. Photoreal joke commercial.
```

Use it as a hard interrupt in a laundry-room or EyeWash Station scene. Do not put it inside
Marble Tick Tack, the Christina material, the Riley moon-morph, or the cowboy-chimp lane.
