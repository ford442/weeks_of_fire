# The Long Way Up — Segment Prompts

Copy-ready prompts for **Grok Imagine** (stills) and **Gemini Omni** (short video clips).  
Script: `notes/scenes/the-long-way-up.md` (~9–12 min, Episode 4 suggestion). Character profile: `characters/suggested-characters.md` (Riley).

## Visual arc

Flat 2 PM county road → sedan crawling at a walker's pace → ordinance sign read like a jury → climbing fire road → hood up in long shadows → one boot / one stiletto on the switchbacks → golden-hour long lens → blue-hour saddle reveal → firelight under a black tower.

## Character continuity

- **Rubella Vale** — drives. Black lace, blood-red lipstick, jet-black hair. Dressed for a funeral in a city ninety miles away.
- **Lillith Vale** — passenger. Matching black lace, matching lipstick. Prosecutes every conversation.
- **Riley** — sun-bleached blonde, light green halter top, amethyst pendant, 40-liter pack, trail-worn. Calm, specific, never theatrical.
- **Vehicle** — dusty sedan, sun-faded paint, one headlight ring duller than the other.
- **From Segment E onward:** both Vales wear **one trail boot and one stiletto**. Never corrected, never mentioned.
- **The gate is never closed** in any shot after Segment C.
- **Hour discipline:** every prompt names its light. No two adjacent segments may read as the same time of day — that is the structural joke of the whole scene.

## A — 2:10 PM — The Traffic Stop That Isn't One (hero)

**Grok Imagine**
```
Cinematic wide film still, flat brutal overhead 2 PM summer light, no shadows to hide in. A sun-bleached blonde woman in a light green halter top with an amethyst pendant and a large trail backpack walks the faded center line of an empty two-lane county road through dry gold California hills. A dusty sedan crawls alongside her at exact walking pace, two women in black lace visible inside, one leaning out the passenger window. Heat shimmer off the asphalt, no shoulder, no other cars, telephone poles receding to the horizon. Deadpan procedural comedy staging, photoreal, 35mm, Grok Imagine style
```

**Gemini Omni**
```
10-second slow tracking video alongside a woman with a backpack walking the center line of an empty county road in flat 2 PM heat. A dusty sedan pulls even with her and matches her walking speed exactly instead of passing. A woman in black lace leans out the passenger window and speaks. The walker does not stop, does not look over, and answers. Heat shimmer, dry gold hills, no other traffic. Photoreal cinematic, deadpan, no music.
```

## C — 3:25 PM — The Gate (hero)

**Grok Imagine**
```
Cinematic medium-wide film still, hard 3 PM shadows and hanging dust. Three women stand shoulder to shoulder in a row facing a faded official forest-service sign on a steel swing gate across a graded dirt fire road — two in black cocktail lace and heels, one sun-bleached in a green halter and hiking pack. All three read the dense wordy ordinance text with the exact posture of a jury reviewing evidence. A cut padlock hangs re-hung on the chain to look locked. Dry gold hillside, deadpan absurdity of formalwear at a trailhead, photoreal, Grok Imagine style
```

**Gemini Omni**
```
10-second locked-off medium-wide video at a steel gate across a dirt fire road, hard mid-afternoon shadows. Three women stand in a row reading a wordy closure sign aloud like evidence, one in a green halter, two in black lace and heels. One steps forward and examines a padlock without touching it. The driver in black lace walks out of frame, the gate swings open behind them, and nobody closes it. Photoreal cinematic, deadpan comedy, no music.
```

## E — 5:20 PM — Hood Up / The Boots (hero)

**Grok Imagine**
```
Cinematic wide film still, warm 5 PM light with very long shadows across a dirt turnout on a steep fire road. A dusty sedan sits at an angle with its hood up, faint steam. A sun-bleached blonde woman is buried under the hood to the shoulders. A woman in black lace holds a phone flashlight up for her in full daylight with total sincerity. A second woman in black lace sits on a cooler watching. One pair of trail boots sits open on the bumper beside a discarded stiletto. Golden dust, dry hills falling away below, deadpan roadside comedy, photoreal, Grok Imagine style
```

**Grok Imagine — signature image**
```
Cinematic low-angle close film still on a steep dirt fire road in long golden 5 PM light: a woman's two feet mid-stride on an eleven percent grade, one foot in a scuffed tan trail boot and the other in a black stiletto heel, black lace hem above, loose gravel and dust kicked up, the road climbing steeply out of frame behind her. Absurd and completely matter-of-fact, photoreal, shallow depth of field, Grok Imagine style
```

**Gemini Omni**

Two women in black lace divide them — each removes one heel and puts on one boot. One stands and takes two experimental steps uphill in one boot and one stiletto. It works badly. Nobody comments. Photoreal cinematic, deadpan, no music.

## Notes

- **Withhold the score.** Segments A–G have no music at all. Any temp track under the climb kills the payoff at H.
- **The tower must never look operational** in A–G — no lights, no glow, no visible feed line. The single red pulse in H is the only exception in the entire episode and is never explained.
- **Do not restyle the Vales for the hike.** No practical wardrobe, no jackets, no ponytails. Black lace all the way to the summit.
- **Riley never poses.** No wind-in-hair hero shots, no gazing at horizons. She is always doing something specific — reading, walking, wrenching, writing.
