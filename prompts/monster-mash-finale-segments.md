# Monster Mash Finale — Segment Prompts

Copy-ready prompts for **Grok Imagine** (stills) and **Gemini Omni** (short video clips).
Script: `episodes/episode-03/monster-mash-finale.md` (~3:00–3:30). Visible on the site **Suggestions** view.

**Prompt policy:** these are written *long on purpose* — near the practical Grok Imagine
prompt ceiling (~1,000–1,300 characters). Grok holds detail better when the frame is
described in one continuous run: subject → wardrobe → blocking → background layers →
light → lens → grade → style tag. Don't trim them to "clean them up." If a generation
comes back mushy, cut **background layers** first, never wardrobe or lighting.

## Visual arc

Lawn aftermath after snake battle → cold-open sing-us-out dialog → Rubella at silver mic →
cursed Monster Mash party montage → **Amy & Alec return from the hiking sketch and dance in
costume** → **Thriller werewolf moonwalks the perimeter** → parallel Lillith spy-heist in
bedroom → hologram reveal → Rubella in white spy gear → Spy vs. Spy noir outro.

## Character continuity

- **Rubella** — lead singer; hologram on stage, then real Rubella in white trench + white circular hat
- **Lillith** — black trench + black circular hat; bedroom heist during Verse 2
- **Madelin** — giant skull tote; manic encouragement in cold open
- **Qing Rao** — crystal-skull keeper; elegant, minimal dialogue
- **Justine** — younger, dark-haired; on vintage couch with lace trio
- **Tabby cat** — orange trick-or-treat bucket at mic base
- **Defeated giant snake coils** on grass throughout

### Returning cameos (wardrobe locked from the hiking sketch)

- **Amy** — head-to-toe loud REI technical gear: magenta and safety-orange shell, teal
  zip-off convertible pants, headlamp still switched on in her hair, absurd number of
  carabiners clipped to every loop so she *jingles* on every beat. Dances frantically,
  over-committed, arms too wide, checks over her shoulder for Gary.
- **Alec** — heavy oatmeal wool fisherman's sweater, aviator sunglasses at night, the
  park-ranger hat he claims he found on a bear, sharpened walking-stick spear used as a
  dance prop. Dances *barely*: minimal weight shifts, dead-serious face, total conviction.
- **Thriller werewolf** — red-and-black leather varsity jacket with heavy shoulders, single
  white sequined glove, yellow lupine eyes, muzzle mid-transformation. Moonwalks backwards
  in a slow orbit around the mic stand, never breaking eye contact with the lens.

Mix art styles chaotically: photoreal, VHS fuzz, PS1 low-poly, inflatable practical.
