# The Soft Gyre — 30s psychedelic 3D cutaway

Copy-ready prompts for **Grok Imagine** (stills / first frames) and **video**
(Gemini Omni long-take, or Grok `image_to_video` as five 6-second shots).

**Working title:** The Soft Gyre  
**Alts:** Jello Weather · Unscheduled Precipitation · The Anvil That Won't Set

Sibling abstract to *Molten Silver Sphere* (cave + chrome + strobe). This one
lives **above weather**, not inside a cave. The blob is the hero. The cloud deck
is the second character. Sparks and gelatin are the language between them.

**Music lean:** instrumental first — *Porch Light Meridian* patience, *Strange
Light* celestial lawbreak, or a new untitled 64–72 BPM cue. No vocal. One
structural flaw in the picture (same rule as the song bible): **the gyre spins
clockwise while every ejected ribbon rotates the other way**, and nobody in the
frame corrects it.

---

## How to maximize detail (prompt stack)

Do **not** write "psychedelic, trippy, lots of colors, surreal." That burns
token budget and returns mush. Stuff the prompt through these eight slots.
Every new idea you have later drops into **one slot** as a verb + object.

| Slot | What it holds | Example you can swap |
|------|---------------|----------------------|
| **1. LOCK** | The one sentence that never changes | A translucent glowing amorphous gel-body hovers above an anvil-cloud floor and spins. |
| **2. BODY** | Interior 3D anatomy this beat | Nested lava-lamp lobes; a gold glass helix; bubbles that contain miniature cloudscapes. |
| **3. SKIN** | Surface physics this beat | Oil-slick meniscus; taut then dimpled; temporary icosahedron facets that melt. |
| **4. THROW** | What leaves the body | Gold-white sparks + raspberry / lime / ultraviolet jello ribbons and cubes. |
| **5. FLOOR** | What the clouds do when hit | Dent like memory foam; glow from inside; sprout a glass flower; refuse to get wet. |
| **6. SKY LAW** | One thing that is structurally wrong | Sun below the cloud deck; ejecta spin against the gyre; lightning the same color as the gel. |
| **7. CAMERA** | One move only | Slow orbit / push-in / lock-off / tilt from floor to body. Never two moves. |
| **8. MICRO** | 2–4 countable events | "Three sparks write a fading ring. One lime cube bounces. One raspberry cube is absorbed." |

**Rules that keep a stuffed prompt from collapsing**

1. **Events, not adjectives.** "A lime ribbon hits the anvil and the cloud grows a glass flower" survives. "Dreamlike vibrant surreal atmosphere" dies.
2. **Count things.** Three sparks. Two cubes. One flower. Models keep numbers better than "many."
3. **One material contradiction per beat.** Gel that throws fire. Cloud that will not wet. Lightning the color of dessert. Stacking five contradictions = soup.
4. **If a generation comes back mushy, cut SKY LAW poetry and distant weather first.** Never cut BODY, SKIN, or THROW.
5. **Video shots carry one THROW + one FLOOR reaction + one CAMERA.** Put extra MICRO events in the *still* (first frame). The animation should only *continue* what the still already shows — complex stills warp if you also add new actions.
6. **Keep the LOCK sentence at the top of every prompt** so shot-to-shot identity does not drift.

**Psychedelic = materials arguing, not a filter.** Translucent gel / molten gold / neon / jello / thunderhead / glass / sparks. The trip is the physics.

## Visual lock (paste into every prompt)

A single hovering blob of glowing amorphous gel — roughly the size of a small
house — floats a few hundred meters above a continuous floor of anvil
thunderheads at golden-violet dusk. The body is translucent enough to read
internal lobes and a slow gold-glass helix. It spins on a lazy tilted axis.
Gold-white sparks and wet jello-like ejecta leave the equator. The cloud deck
is a second character: dented, internally lit, never rain-wet. Photoreal 3D
cinema, not illustration, not fractal-poster.

**Palette lock:** phosphor gold, raspberry gel, electric lime, ultraviolet
edge-light, warm undersun (the sun is *under* the clouds), crushed-violet
stratosphere.

## 30-second cut (five 6s shots)

Build this as **five first-frame stills**, then animate each still for 6
seconds. Do not try to get all five beats out of one long take unless you are
feeding Gemini Omni the full-run prompt at the bottom.

| Time | Shot | Camera | What happens (MICRO) |
|------|------|--------|----------------------|
| 0:00–0:06 | **A — Hover** | Slow rise from cloud floor to the body | Spin starts. Three sparks peel off the equator and die as rings. |
| 0:06–0:12 | **B — Weather** | Gentle orbit, body left-third | Raspberry + lime jello flung downward. Clouds dent. One cube bounces; one is swallowed and the cloud glows. |
| 0:12–0:18 | **C — Interior** | Push-in through the skin | Nested lobes, gold helix, two bubbles holding tiny cloudscapes. A spark is born *inside* and tunnels out. |
| 0:18–0:24 | **D — Answer** | Lock-off wide-ish | Clouds send a vapor tendril up. It kisses the underside. The gyre wobbles; ejecta still spin the wrong way. |
| 0:24–0:30 | **E — Soft set** | Slow pull-back | Spin eases. Leftover sparks write one fading halo. A single glass flower remains on the anvil. Undersun flares once. |

**Structural flaw (hold for the whole runtime):** gyre clockwise; every spark,
ribbon, and cube counter-rotates.

## Detail bank (steal from here when you want more)

Drop any line into the matching slot. Do not dump the whole bank into one prompt.

### BODY
- Nested lava-lamp lobes in raspberry, lime, and smoked gold, rising against the spin.
- A gold-glass double helix turning slower than the outer skin, like a clock inside jelly.
- Subsurface convection cells that brighten when a spark is about to be born.
- Two or three trapped bubbles; each contains a miniature anvil-cloud, lit from below.
- A faint icosahedral "bone" of harder gel that melts whenever it reaches the equator.
- Internal lightning the same phosphor-gold as the sparks — never white.
- A dark seed at the exact center, the size of a fist, that does not spin.

### SKIN
- Oil-slick meniscus ridges that crawl toward the equator then fling.
- Temporary hard facets (icosahedron) that hold for two seconds then slump back to gel.
- Dimples that form under departing sparks, then heal with a wet click of light.
- A thin dry "sugar crust" on the night side that cracks and becomes more sparks.
- Translucent skin so the helix is readable, but the limb is rim-lit ultraviolet.
- Slow breathing: volume up 8%, down 8%, out of phase with the spin.

### THROW
- Gold-white sparks that write two or three cursive loops, then go out without smoke.
- Raspberry gelatin cubes with rounded corners; they tumble, stretch into ribbons, snap back.
- Electric-lime ribbons that behave like slow lightning and refuse to break.
- Ultraviolet droplets that are only visible as they pass in front of a cloud.
- Tiny daughter-blobs that try to become new gyres, fail, and fall back into the parent.
- One spark that lands on a cloud and becomes a glass flower instead of going out.

### FLOOR (clouds)
- Memory-foam dents that keep the cube's shape for a beat, then forget.
- Internal glow (raspberry or lime) when a cube is absorbed — the cloud becomes a lantern.
- Glass flowers / sugar-crystal anemones where sparks plant themselves.
- A slow whirlpool opening under the gyre, never quite becoming an eye.
- Clouds refuse rain; impacts splash as light, not water.
- One anvil develops a flat glass "table" that the next cube skids across.

### SKY LAW
- The sun is **below** the cloud deck; we only see it as a warm underglow and a late flare through a hole.
- Ejecta rotate against the gyre.
- Lightning is dessert-colored (raspberry / lime / gold), never photographic white.
- No ground, no city, no aircraft, no people — only weather and the body.
- Stars are visible in the violet stratosphere even though it is dusk.

## Music / cutaway notes

- **Diegetic world:** none. This is a pure visual poem / EyeWash "late movie"
  insert — *Strange Light* kinship (sky lawbreak, one witness, except here the
  witness is the camera).
- **Do not** put Rubella or the Two in this. Faces will flatten the materials.
- **Do not** score it with Ultra Screech or Recycling Bin Rebellion. Wrong job.
- If it needs a station card, Continuity Voice only, after the flare:
  *"This weather is unscheduled. Your altitude is not our department."*

## Generate order

1. Shot **A** still (this is the identity lock). Reject if the body is opaque,
   on the ground, or looks like a spaceship / brain / planet.
2. Derive B–E stills from A (same body, same palette, same cloud floor).
3. Animate each still for 6s with the short video line. Prefer camera-only if
   a still is already dense (especially Shot C).
4. Concatenate A→E. Do not re-encode.

If you only generate one thing first: **Shot A still.** Everything else is a
costume change on that object.
