# Episode 4 – Scenes

**Logline:** The morning after the portal, the HOA reclassifies cosmic horror as seasonal décor.

**Duration:** ~6 min (cold open ~90s + meeting 4–6 min; cut 911 if long)  
**Location:** Cul-de-sac at dawn / HOA clubhouse at night  
**Tone:** Deadpan bureaucracy. Nobody is frightened. Brad is the only person having a genuine emotional experience, and it is about a bin.  
**Guests:** The Two (Vivienne & Liliane Vale) standing at the back wall in black lace, out of place on purpose, non-voting.  
**Music:** Sunrise waltz under the cold open; Muzak + unresolved theremin in the meeting; optional *Rinse Cycle* ident under the Pizza Guy exit.
