# Episode 4 — Synopsis

**Logline:** The morning after the portal, the HOA reclassifies cosmic horror as seasonal décor.

**Brad’s tag:** The bin is still the wrong blue — and the purple light has a parking permit.

---

Dawn on the cul-de-sac. Dew on lawns. A few plastic skeletons still upright. Someone is power-washing a sigil off a driveway as if it were pollen. Karen, bathrobe over last night’s clipboard, announces that the purple light is now a decorative accent and does not require a permit. Brad, holding swatches, would like the minutes to reflect that his recycling bin is still the wrong shade of democratic blue. A kid bikes past with a serpent scale like a trading card. The Pizza Guy from Halloween is now delivering breakfast burritos and does not change expression.

The optional cutaway is the 911 call: Karen reports existential zoning; the operator asks if anyone is bleeding; “Democracy is bleeding, Steven.” Transfer loop until the doorbell. Do not play this as panic. Play it as hold music.

Night. Emergency session of the Homeowners Association. Dale gavels a cracked gavel. Item one is still the bin. Item two is still the bin under three lighting conditions. Item three is the light. Through the blinds: purple, then bruise-colored, then something that would require a variance if anyone measured it. Patricia raises her hand about height. Gary Johnson has not looked up since 2019. At the back wall — not sitting, non-voting — Vivienne and Liliane Vale in black lace, holding wine that did not come from this building. Biscuit’s bandana is currently and inexplicably purple. Karen wins by renaming. Dale’s authority was the gavel; Biscuit steals it. The meeting does not adjourn. It persists.

The Pizza Guy appears in the doorway with the order nobody placed. Extra napkins are in the bag. Eight bars of *Rinse Cycle* optional under the exit.
