## What the pictures already say

1. **Tandem = partnership, not a proposal prop.** Front seat / back seat is Rubella and Lillith without a speech. “Bicycle built for two” stays the flip cue because the line names the machine they already share.
2. **Class is the lyric, played on the curb.** Broken hansom, resigned horse, beggar with one wilted daisy, closed carriage that peeks and slams. Stylish landau lean is the answering chorus. If the middle third gets only pretty, the song becomes a postcard.
3. **Time will not settle.** Field is now. Fleet Street is then. HD Victorian is modern photography of a walking past. Nitrate scratch is the pipeline admitting it is film. Soft return says London was a visit.
4. **They stay wrong on purpose.** Subject lock: modern black lace, no corset, no gigot, no hats on the singers. Everyone else is 1890–96 as class code. Continuity is still a character.
5. **The bike is alive; the city is rented.** Too bright in monochrome. Flower girl freezes because her inventory rode past. Wedding bouquet leans. Wilted curb daisy is the dead twin of the living frame.

Song afterlife (use lightly, do not costume): first computer-sung tune (Bell Labs, 1961), later HAL's last human memory. Generated faces singing that song while the picture cannot pick nitrate or HD is enough. No visor on Fleet Street. A bicycle-bell ping on the 4:03 silence bite is the most that register should ever get.

Inverse of the mountain-lion field: lion stays in the flowers and goes *in*; Daisy starts in the same kind of field and rides *out*, then home. Hanshaw is the indoor heart. Daisy is the outdoor heart. Do not stack them as twins.

## Unreal insert: indoor river building

Not whipped cream. A wrong interior.

They ride off the edge-of-London road, through trees, into a riverside building that failed to keep the river outside. Mill / pump house / boathouse / orangery / warehouse — brick or stone, too much glass, one room longer than it is wide. Old single panes, waviness, putty, a few cracked lights. Trees scrape the glass. Inside, a shallow river with a current, ankle to mid-shin, boards or stones showing through. Drips from the sash hit the indoor stream on the waltz (one-two-*three*). Side light, green from trees, silver from water, lace darker where it wets.

This is not the Weeks on Fire apartment block in a wig. Not the laundry leak. Not the lion's ocean act. Not a haunt.

**Ride in, maybe two steps off, ride out.** The song is still a bicycle song. The tandem does not become a boat.
