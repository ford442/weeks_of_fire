## Concept

Screeching glam metal energy as a hyper-saturated Japanese TV commercial for an absurd heavy-metal shampoo. Shot as if running on the EyeWash Station TV or blasting from the laundry-room vending machine.

**Glam-Sham-Poo** is both:
- The company / neon region (**Glamora** in **Sham-Poo Prefecture**)
- A famous college (the kind of place where the dorms have better pyrotechnics than the chemistry labs and the student handbook has a whole section on “authorized volume”)

**Product**: Ultra Screech Formula – “Heavy Metal Hair Detox Shampoo”. Turns split ends into power chords. Hair so loud it violates HOA noise ordinances *and* the college noise curfew.

**Cast**:
- **Kenji “Shred” Sato** – Official brand ambassador / alumnus of Glam-Sham-Poo College. Big hair, leather fringe, pure hype.
- **Rubella** – Deadpan endorsement. Exhausted cabaret energy.
- **Lillith** – Chaotic high-energy endorsement. Starts the volume, keeps raising it.

## Production Notes – Stills → Video Pipeline

This is the same workflow we can use for lots of other cutaways:

1. Generate strong Grok Imagine stills (performance, product, environment)
2. Write / generate the audio (spoken or full Minimax track)
3. Assemble as timed slideshow or simple motion (Ken Burns / subtle zooms if needed)
4. **Lipsync strategy** (because post-facto lipsync is hard):
   - Prefer **beat-sync** and visual energy over perfect mouth shapes
   - Keep singers **non-visible or off-camera** for the heavy vocal lines when possible
   - When we do want on-camera vocals, only put the specific lines we care about syncing live (short, punchy, or already rhythmic)
   - Everything else can be treated as performance / attitude / dance under the track

This keeps the cutaways fast to make and still feels musical.
