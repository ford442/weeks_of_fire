# Cat POV — Silent Witness Cutaway

**Runtime:** ~70–90 seconds  
**Dialog:** none  
**Music:** [Deep Space Drift](../songs/Deep_Space_Drift.md) (weightless pedal-steel; not the Halloween battle cue)  
**Continuity:** the same sleek black cat with yellow eyes from Episode 3 (`episodes/episode-03/scenes.md`)  
**Prompts:** `prompts/cat-pov-segments.md`  
**Catalog:** `content/cutaways/cat-pov.json`

This is the proof that the series has a **pure-visual lane**: no Continuity Voice, no HOA minutes, no Vale sisters. The cat does not understand the plot and does not need to. We watch what it watches.

---

## Logline

A black cat walks Halloween, witnesses the laser lawn, clocks the Pizza Guy from ankle height, and is still on the driveway the morning the purple light becomes décor.

---

## Rules

1. **Zero lines.** If a human speaks, they are off-mic or through glass. We do not subtitle them.
2. **Cat height unless the crane is the joke.** Start low. The Episode 3 pull-back can happen, but it starts from fur, not from a jib.
3. **Same animal.** Sleek black cat, yellow eyes, tail as weather vane. Not a second cat. Not a cute cartoon.
4. **Sound design carries it.** Footfalls, a single interrogative chirp, distant lasers, porch *click*, burrito-bag rustle, dawn birds, power-washer. Music is a pad, not a theme song with a chorus.
5. **The cat is a witness, not a protagonist.** It does not save anyone. It knocks one object if it wants (the union sign is traditional). It leaves when it is done.
