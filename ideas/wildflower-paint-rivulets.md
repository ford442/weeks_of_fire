# Wildflower paint rivulets

Shared ground language for two field cutaways. Not a new short.

**Cutaways:** Daisy Bell (field bookends) · Mountain Lion Wildflower Field (drag + far shore)  
**Farm:** [#22](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/22) · not [#42](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/42)  
**Date:** 2026-09-05

Paint runs along the ground in several wet colors **between** the wildflowers. It does not coat the petals. It does not turn the meadow into a map. It behaves like someone spilled enamel uphill and the field decided to keep it.

Daisy rides *out* of this field. The lion walks *in*. Same dirt grammar. Different verb. Worlds still do not mix: no tandem in the multi-lion clearing, no leash on Fleet Street.

---

## What it is

Thin bright rivulets in the soil and last year's stems: ultramarine, sap green, cadmium yellow, alizarin, a little white that stays milky instead of mixing clean. They follow existing ruts, vole paths, tire lines, paw lines. They pool in hoof prints and bicycle tracks. They never climb the blooms.

The flowers stay flowers. The paint is weather that forgot it was paint.

This is the silly-unreal that breaks the documentary meadow without borrowing the whipped-cream ocean from the other video. Color on the ground is enough.

---

## Shared locks

- **Between the flowers, not on them.** Painted / watercolor petal edges already exist in the lion botanical-surreal toolkit. Rivulets are a different layer. Do not collapse them.
- **Wet, not graphic.** No hard stripes, no logo, no rainbow-bar. Pigment in dirt. Edges feather into dust.
- **Gravity still works.** Paint runs downhill, into ruts, toward water if water is in frame. It does not orbit. It does not spell.
- **One substance rule at any tideline.** If paint meets sea (lion) or indoor river (Daisy), it thins into the water as color in the current — not a second liquid sitting on top.
- **Do not steal the hero lie.** Daisy's living tandem stays the joke of that picture. The lion's deadpan walk stays the joke of that picture. Paint is garnish that can be read in a wide.

## Daisy Bell — where it lives

Field bookends only, plus whatever dirt the tires carry.

| Cue | Paint |
|-----|--------|
| C0–C2 color field | Rivulets already in the meadow. Tandem rolls through; tracks fill brighter. |
| C3 first grain | Color drains from the *world*; rivulets are the last chroma to go, then they are just wet scratches in the nitrate. |
| Fleet Street / HD Victorian | Mostly gone. Optional: one gutter streak the same ultramarine, as if the field leaked into London. One streak max. |
| Indoor river room (see `ideas/daisy-bell-themes.md`) | Drips from single-pane glass pick up a color. The indoor current carries a faint ribbon. Do not fill the room with pigment. |
| C16–C18 return field | Rivulets again. Wet tire print in the pollen. Proof they left. |

The bike may shed a petal onto a rivulet. The petal floats a few inches and stops. That is the whole joke.

## Mountain Lion — where it lives

Field corridor and far shore. Not a new destination.

| Block | Paint |
|-------|--------|
| A–B insistence / pull | Thin color in the realistic park dirt, easy to miss. |
| C painted architecture | Rivulets denser in the soil while some *blooms* go watercolor. Two layers, readable apart. |
| D field becomes sea | Paint reaches the tideline and becomes tint in the foam. No enamel slick sitting on open water. |
| E crossing | Optional hairline of color in the bloom-causeway, like a wet seam. Do not stripe the ocean. |
| F–H far shore + clearing | Rivulets return thicker and stranger, matching the bigger painted flowers. Other lions ignore them. One paw print fills with yellow and nobody cares. |

Leash may drag a brief wet line. The cat does not sniff the paint.

## Imagine prompt fragment (drop into field wides)

```
Photoreal ground between the wildflowers: thin wet rivulets of enamel-like pigment in ultramarine, sap green, cadmium, and alizarin running along the dirt and old stems, pooling in small ruts and prints, never climbing the petals, edges feathered into dust, gravity-true, silly but quiet, Weeks on Fire field language.
```

Generate one **ground-only plate** first (no hero, just flowers + rivulets) and lock the look before dropping it under the tandem or the leash. If the model paints the blooms instead of the dirt, the plate failed.
