# Mountain Lion Wildflower Field

POV musical cutaway. A mountain lion on a leash **insists** on showing us a field instead of whatever we were doing. Flowers escalate from real meadow scale into botanical surrealism (impossible variety, oversized blooms, some painted). Arrival: a sunlit clearing already full of other mountain lions looking at the flowers and the light.

## Locked choices

- **POV leash.** Camera is the walker being pulled. Leash reads in frame. Do not cut to an unexplained third-person hero unless expanding later.
- **Deadpan lion.** The cat never acknowledges the absurdity. Focused “this is my walk.” Humor is dignity vs scale.
- **Two-bed fade, same BPM (110).** Do not run 2 minutes of drag + 1 minute of field. Target **~1:50–2:15** total.
- **Not metal.** Snake-battle guitar stays on that cutaway. This bed is careful digital FM chirps + minimal soft kick, then brilliant light on arrival.
- **Botanical surrealism, not horror.** No gore, no attack, no “predator jumps the camera” gag. Other lions lounge. They are already here.
- **Do not dump this onto #42** (Ep4 production hub). This is a short cutaway packet.

## Visual arc

1. **Insistence** — lion looks back (or keeps walking) with “you’re coming.” Realistic park / trees. Purple lupines starting to read large.
2. **Deeper pull** — multi-color oversized lupines, motion, golden light, leash taut.
3. **Botanical surrealism** — unreal number of species; some blooms photoreal, some painted / watercolor edges; flowers as architecture / tunnel.
4. **Arrival** — sun-drenched clearing; other mountain lions already there; original lion still on the leash in the foreground.
5. **Gratifying hold** — light almost stuns; cats + big field; no more pulling.

## Botanical surrealism toolkit (for agents expanding stills)

1. Hybrid media — photoreal petals next to painted / watercolor blooms in the same frame.
2. Impossible taxonomy — too many species at once.
3. Scale as architecture — stems as columns, petals as rooms, path as tunnel.
4. Soft biomorphism — optional, quiet; no cartoon faces unless asked.
5. Light as generative force — golden light that seems to grow the painted sections.
6. Chance texture — soft pressed / scraped petal edges on the painted set.

Reusable Imagine prompt:

```
Cinematic botanical surrealism, immersive dense field overflowing with an almost impossible number of different oversized flowers of every type. Some fully photoreal, others with delicate painted watercolor and soft brush-stroked edges mixed in the same scene. Towering lupines, bluebells, poppies, roses, foxgloves, lilies, dahlias and fantastical blooms forming a surrounding living architecture / tunnel. Golden sunlight that feels generative, rich textures, dreamlike yet grounded, Weeks on Fire visual style.
```
