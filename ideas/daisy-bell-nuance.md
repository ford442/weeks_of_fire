Admission rule for anything on this page — every entry is one of three things:

1. a **physical object in frame** (generatable, promptable),
2. a **continuity link** to another cue, or
3. a **timing hit** on the ¾ pulse.

No atmosphere, no mood words, nothing that only exists in the edit note. If a nuance cannot be pointed at in a still, it does not belong here.

Nothing on this page introduces mystical register, Edwardian-ghost destiny, Glam puncture, bird flock, cowboy chimp, marble cabinet, cream ocean, lion, or Christina.

## Notes for generation

- The **bare foot** and the **flower count** are the two cheapest wins — both survive treatment flips and neither needs a shot of its own. Lock them into the subject-lock fragments in `src/data/daisyBell.ts` rather than re-prompting per still.
- **Wrong-order chroma drain** (C3) is an edit/grade move, not a generation move. Generate the field plate at full color and pull green→blue→red in post.
- **Same-geometry proof** at C12: whichever way the climax goes, one physical feature must be identical across the flip — street pane or river-room pane. If the room takes the climax, that proof moves onto the glass, which the themes doc already permits.
- Several entries (C6 glow bleed, C13 lamp with no cord, C16 wet-to-dry) are more reliable as **grade or comp** than as prompt text. Generate clean; add the wrongness after.
- The girl is ordinary. Reject lace, red lips, or a seat on the living tandem.
